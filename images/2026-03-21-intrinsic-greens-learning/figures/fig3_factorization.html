Core Result

Fubini Factorization: d-Dim → d × 1D

separable source + separable kernel → d-dimensional integral splits into d independent 1D integrals

\(\hat{f}(\zeta^1, \zeta^2)\), hover to explore

2D Integral: naively \(O(N^2)\)

=

1D factor: \(\int G \cdot \phi_1 \, d\zeta^1\)

×

1D factor: \(\int G \cdot \phi_2 \, d\zeta^2\)

Each 1D: \(O(N)\), product = same result ✓

(1)\(u(\xi) = \iint G(\xi,\zeta)\cdot\hat{f}(\zeta)\,d\zeta^1 d\zeta^2\) , d-dimensional, naively \(O(N^d)\)
(2)\(\hat{f}(\zeta) = \sum_r w_r\) \(\phi_{r1}(\zeta^1)\cdot\phi_{r2}(\zeta^2)\), \(G(\xi,\zeta) \approx \sum_k \gamma_k\) \(G_{k1}(\xi^1,\zeta^1)\cdot G_{k2}(\xi^2,\zeta^2)\) , rank-R, rank-K
(3)\(u(\xi) = \sum_{k,r} \gamma_k w_r\) \(\bigl[\int G_{k1}(\xi^1,\zeta^1)\phi_{r1}(\zeta^1)\,d\zeta^1\bigr]\cdot\bigl[\int G_{k2}(\xi^2,\zeta^2)\phi_{r2}(\zeta^2)\,d\zeta^2\bigr]\) ← Fubini
(4)\(u(\xi) = \sum_{k,r} \gamma_k w_r\) \(I_{kr1}(\xi^1)\)\(\cdot\)\(I_{kr2}(\xi^2)\) , product of 1D integrals only ✓
