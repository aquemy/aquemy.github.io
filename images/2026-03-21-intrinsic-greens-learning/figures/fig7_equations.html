The IGL Framework: Step by Step

1
The PDE Reframing
Architecture
▼
Instead of: u ≈ f_θ(x) (direct approximation) IGL models: Lu = f (L = differential operator, f = learned) Solution: u(ξ) = ∫ G(ξ,ζ) f(ζ) dζ (Green's convolution)
This is an architectural choice, not a physical claim. The PDE formulation simultaneously produces: (a) a decomposable tensor representation, (b) a smoothing inductive bias, (c) linearity in source weights enabling the two-stage split.
🔑 Modeling f (the source) rather than u makes Stage 2 a linear problem.
2
The Encoder: Coordinate Discovery
Stage 1
▼
Ψ : ℝᴰ → ℝᵈ (any differentiable arch: MLP, CNN, …) ξ = Ψ(x) · diag(g₁,…,gd) ← gates gⱼ ∈ [0,1] Goal: find ξ such that f̂(ξ) admits low-rank tensor factorization
The encoder seeks Korobov-smooth coordinates, not isometric embedding. Tensor rank is coordinate-dependent: a radial function is rank-1 in polar but may require cross-terms in rotated Cartesian frames.
🔑 IGL finds a task-optimal chart, not necessarily isometric coordinates.
3
Rank-R Source Decomposition
Representation
▼
f̂(ζ) = Σᵣ₌₁ᴿ wᵣ ∏ⱼ₌₁ᵈ φᵣⱼ(ζʲ) φᵣⱼ(ζʲ) = exp(−(ζʲ − μᵣⱼ)² / 2σ²) ← RBF at anchor μᵣⱼ Parameters: w ∈ ℝᴿ (Stage 2) μ ∈ ℝᴿˣᵈ (Stage 1)
CP (Canonical Polyadic) decomposition: each rank-1 term is a product of d univariate functions. Rank R controls expressivity. Group Lasso prunes inactive terms.
🔑 Separability of f̂ is the hypothesis that enables Fubini. The encoder's job is to make it true.
4
Fubini Factorization (Central Result)
O(KRd)
▼
G(ξ,ζ) ≈ Σₖ γₖ ∏ⱼ Gₖⱼ(ξʲ,ζʲ) (rank-K separable approx) u(ξ) = Σₖᵣ γₖwᵣ ∏ⱼ ∫Gₖⱼ(ξʲ,ζʲ)φᵣⱼ(ζʲ)dζʲ ← Fubini = Σₖᵣ γₖwᵣ ∏ⱼ Iₖᵣⱼ(ξʲ) Cost: O(KRd) per sample K = O(log ε⁻¹) dim-independent
Fubini applies because the integrand is a product of functions each depending on a single coordinate ζʲ. The d-dimensional integral splits into d independent 1D integrals.
🔑 Core computational result: integration cost is O(KRd), linear in intrinsic dimension d.
5
Compensated Source + Null Space
Completeness
▼
u(ξ) = Σₖᵣ γₖwᵣ ∏ⱼ Iₖᵣⱼ(ξʲ) + Σ_{|β|≤p} cβ ∏ⱼ (ξʲ)^βⱼ particular solution (G∗f̂) null-space polynomials f̂(ζ) = f̃(ζ)·√|g(ζ)| ← metric volume absorbed implicitly
Operators with spectral gap (Helmholtz, harmonic oscillator) need no augmentation. The metric factor √|g| and operator coupling correction are both absorbed into f̂, never computed explicitly.
🔑 Double compensation: f̂ absorbs both metric volume and operator coupling, entirely implicit.
6
Variable Projection Algorithm
Algorithm
▼
Stage 2 (inner): w* = argmin_w ‖Φw − y‖² exact lstsq Stage 1 (outer): min_θ ‖y − Φ(θ)w*(θ)‖² gradient descent Envelope theorem: ∂L/∂w = 0 at optimum → ∇_θ L reflects only coordinate quality, no source confound
Solving Stage 2 to optimality at each outer step means the encoder gradient carries a pure signal: are these coordinates good for fitting? No co-adaptation possible.
🔑 VP prevents dimensional collapse by decoupling coordinate discovery from source fitting.
