Framework

Three Learning Problems on ℳ

classification · regression · reconstruction, all share the same manifold geometry

Classification: learn a partition of ℳ into discrete classes, boundaries curve with the manifold geometry. Regression: learn a smooth scalar field u : ℳ → ℝ, iso-contours follow geodesics. Reconstruction: infer ℳ from sparse, noisy observations, the encoder Ψ recovers the embedding.
