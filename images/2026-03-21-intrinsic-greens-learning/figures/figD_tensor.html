Source Factorization

Rank-R Tensor Decomposition of the Source f̂(ξ)

each term is a product of univariate functions, enabling Fubini's theorem

The source f̂ is approximated as a sum of R rank-1 terms, each an outer product of d univariate functions φᵣⱼ. Rank 1 gives a single axis-aligned bump (separable). Higher rank adds more blobs, each still axis-aligned per term. Fubini's theorem applies term-by-term to all ranks, the cost is O(KRd), linear in intrinsic dimension d.
