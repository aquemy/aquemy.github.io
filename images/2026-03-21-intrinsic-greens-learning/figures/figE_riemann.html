The Laplacian Changes on a Curved Manifold

flat ℝ²: Δ = ∂²/∂x₁² + ∂²/∂x₂² · curved ℳ: Δ_g = (1/√|g|) ∂ᵢ(√|g| gⁱʲ ∂ⱼ)

Left (flat ℝ²): isotropic metric g = I, straight geodesics, circular level sets, standard Laplacian Δ = ∂²/∂x₁²+∂²/∂x₂². The Green's function separates cleanly: G(x₁,x₂) = G₁(x₁)·G₂(x₂). Right (curved ℳ): position-dependent metric g(p) (shown as differently-shaped ellipses at p₁,p₂), curved geodesics, tilted/distorted level sets, tangent space T_pℳ as local flat approximation. The Laplace-Beltrami operator Δ_g mixes coordinate derivatives, G is no longer separable.
