Key Insight

The Encoder Seeks an Approximately Flat Chart

Ψ transforms ℳ so the Euclidean Laplacian approximates Δ_g — residual absorbed by the compensated source

The encoder Ψ does not need to be isometric. It finds coordinates where the manifold is approximately flat — so solving Δu=f̂ (Euclidean) is equivalent to solving Δ_gu=f on the original manifold, up to a correction term absorbed into the learned source f̂. The compensated source handles both the metric volume √|g| and operator coupling entirely implicitly.
