Core Obstacle

Why Curvature Breaks Separability

G(x₁,x₂) = G₁(x₁)·G₂(x₂) on flat ℝ² — but this fails on any curved manifold

Left (flat ℝ²): G(x₁,x₂) = e^{−x₁²}·e^{−x₂²} — circular contours, both cross-sections have identical shape (Gaussian). Factorization holds exactly: Fubini collapses 2D → two 1D integrals. Right (curved ℳ): G depends on geodesic distance — contours are tilted ellipses, cross-sections G(·,0) and G(0,·) have different shapes. No axis-aligned factorization. This is the obstacle IGL's encoder is tasked to overcome.
