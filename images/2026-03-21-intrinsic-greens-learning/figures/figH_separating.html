The Encoder's Dual Task

Ψ Simultaneously Flattens ℳ and Separates the Operator

the encoder is trained so that G(ξ¹,ξ²) ≈ G₁(ξ¹)·G₂(ξ²) in the learned coordinates

Before training: random encoder — G has cross-coupling, tilted contours, cross-sections G(·,0) and G(0,·) have different shapes. Fubini cannot be applied. The encoder learns: via the VP gradient signal, Ψ is pushed toward coordinates where G is approximately separable. The gradient encodes both geometric flattening and operator separation. After training: G's contours are nearly circular and axis-aligned, both cross-sections match in shape — G ≈ G₁·G₂, residual rank drops toward 1, and Fubini's factorization becomes valid.
