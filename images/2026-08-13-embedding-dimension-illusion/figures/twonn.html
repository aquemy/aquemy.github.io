Ten 2-D islands (teal) plus a growing population of isolated tokens (orange) scattered between them in a 512-dimensional ambient space, the configuration every real vocabulary has. The islands never change, and their measured dimension stays at 2. The global estimate more than doubles, because the isolated tokens' neighbor distances are between-island distances, concentrated by high dimension. Numbers are precomputed with the standard TwoNN fit (top 10% of ratios discarded) on the actual 512-D configuration; the drawing is a 2-D schematic of it.
