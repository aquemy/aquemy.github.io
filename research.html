Summary

I work at the intersection of machine learning, mathematics and software engineering. My research interest is differential geometry for AI: the shape of the spaces a model computes in, and what that shape says about what the model does.

  • Now. Postdoctoral researcher at the Center for Credible AI, Warsaw University of Technology, since November 2026, on differential geometry and mechanistic interpretability of language models.
  • Research. Geometry, flows and SDE/PDE for the interpretability of AI models. Inverse problems on Riemannian manifolds. Machine-checked mathematics.
  • Thesis. End-to-end classification in unstructured spaces, applied to judicial decisions. Summary
  • Community. Member of the Eloquence AI Community of Experts. ML4Good and BlueDot Impact technical AI safety courses.
  • CVs. Research CV · Engineering CV

Current work

My main object of study is the flow a language model induces on its vocabulary as a token moves through the layers. My research question is what equation of motion this flow follows, and what it preserves.

  • The depth flow of token representations is nonlinear and does not descend its own density NeurReps Workshop @ NeurIPS 2026, Proceedings Track (PMLR)
  • Measuring optimal transport in transformer depth arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.00748
  • A hub of short rows inflates intrinsic dimension estimation of token embeddings arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.29702

I am interested in how much of a model's width a prediction actually uses. My research question is whether the directions that matter for one prediction are the same across contexts, and which tools can see them.

  • Function lives where variance doesn't: task-weighted charts of a language model's computation arXiv preprint arXiv:2609.18989
  • The dimensional cost of context in language models ICLR 2027, under review

In preparation: a per-layer certificate for when a binarized reading of an MLP is faithful.

I am interested in inverse problems on Riemannian manifolds. My research question is how to recover the source of a field from a few scattered measurements, on a manifold known only through samples.

  • Intrinsic Green's Learning: supervised learning on manifolds via inverse PDE AI & PDE Workshop @ ICLR 2026
  • Riemannian geometry for pre-trained language model embeddings GroundLM Workshop @ EMNLP 2026

In preparation: sparse field reconstruction (invited, Machine Learning: Science and Technology), how many coordinates a task needs, IGL on the SPD manifold for cross-subject BCI, IGL for contextual word embeddings.

Writing: Intrinsic Green's Learning · MHALM, a geometric language model for the Parameter Golf challenge

I am interested in proofs written with an LLM and checked by the Lean kernel. My research question is how to keep an honest record of what is proved and what is assumed.

Tools: Conventional Knowledge Commits · ClaimGraph · Measure-to-Measure Transformers in Lean 4 · write-up. In preparation: the CKC paper.
